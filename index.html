About

Fifteen years of designing systems that measure what donors and management need to know

I'm Senior Statistician and functional Head of the Corporate Performance Section at the Geothermal Development Company (GDC), a Kenyan state corporation under the Ministry of Energy. I've been with GDC since 2010 and in this role since 2015, running the organisation's monitoring and evaluation framework against its strategic plan using a Balanced Scorecard cascade, and monitoring three flagship geothermal projects: Menengai, Baringo-Silali, and Suswa.

My donor portfolio spans AFD, KfW, EIB, AfDB, USAID, JICA and GRMF financed programmes. For the Baringo-Silali project's Paka component, I designed the full M&E system from scratch, results framework, indicator matrix, data collection tools, data flow protocols, reporting templates and QA procedures, which is the first piece in the portfolio below. I work daily in Python, R, Stata, Power BI, Tableau, DHIS2, Kobo Toolbox and SQL, and I'm a native Kiswahili speaker, fluent in English.
