About

Fifteen years of designing systems that measure what donors and management need to know

I'm Senior Statistician and functional Head of the Corporate Performance Management Division at the Geothermal Development Company (GDC), a Kenyan state corporation under the Ministry of Energy. I've been with GDC since 2010 and have led this function since 2015, supervising a team of ten across the Nairobi and Nakuru offices. I provide technical guidance to analysts and departmental focal persons on indicators, monitoring tools, data quality and reporting, and I run the performance review forums with departmental heads, directorate heads and the executive.

I run the corporate performance management cycle, cascading the strategic plan into departmental targets and tracking implementation monthly across 25 departments, the second piece in the portfolio below. I also designed and run the M&E systems for three flagship geothermal projects, Menengai, Baringo-Silali, and Suswa, monitoring implementation through quarterly field verification visits to each site.

Of the three, the Baringo-Silali system, covering the project's Paka component, is the one I've put in the portfolio below: a results framework and indicator matrix, the data collection tools and flow protocols that feed them, the reporting templates they roll up into, and the QA procedures that keep the numbers trustworthy. AFD, KfW, EIB, AfDB, USAID, JICA and GRMF have been development partners for the three geothermal projects, and the systems I designed provide the data that goes into their reports. I'm proficient in Excel, Google Sheets, Python, R, SPSS, Tableau and Power BI, and I have working knowledge of KoboToolbox and DHIS2. I'm a native Kiswahili speaker, fluent in English.
